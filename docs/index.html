📜 About the Game

The Deep Dark is a roguelike dungeon crawler where you navigate procedurally-generated maze-like ruins. Each level presents new challenges as you delve deeper into the darkness, hunted by relentless enemies.

🎮 How to Play

  • Use arrow keys or WASD to move through the dungeon
  • Collect 🔥 torches to increase your light radius
  • Collect 💫 power orbs to gain pulses (special abilities)
  • Press SPACE or P to activate a pulse and temporarily see all enemies
  • Reach the bottom of each level to advance deeper
  • Survive as long as possible and climb the global leaderboard!

🎮 Game Modes

Player Mode: Standard gameplay where you must survive and descend as far as possible before your torch runs out or you're caught by enemies.

Dungeon Master Mode: Unlock with access key to explore dungeons without time pressure. Use this to design and test maps!

🏆 Leaderboard

Climb the global leaderboard by reaching greater depths. Your score is determined by how far you descend before being eliminated.

All scores are tracked in real-time and persist across sessions. Will you become a legendary survivor?

  • Firebase Firestore for leaderboard
